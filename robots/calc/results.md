## 2. Static stability

- Lateral tip-over angle (arms stowed): **27.5 deg**
- Lateral tip-over, one arm fully out to the downhill side with fruit: **25.9 deg** (CoG shift 20 mm)
- Longitudinal tip-over angle: **44.7 deg**
- Florida vegetable fields are < 2 deg; the bed shoulder can tilt one side by ~6 deg if a tyre rides up on it. Required margin: tip angle >= 3x worst operating tilt (6 deg -> 18 deg). Result: PASS.
- Software limit: arms may not extend beyond 0.6 m lateral when IMU roll > 8 deg.
