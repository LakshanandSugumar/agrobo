# Electrical architecture and safety (AB-1 + HM-1, rev A)

Drawings: `electrical/E-01_power_single_line.pdf`, `E-02_safety_circuit.pdf`, `E-03_data_comms.pdf` (SVG too). Wire list: `electrical/harness.csv`. All three are regenerated by `electrical/build_schematics.py`.

v1 deliberately uses **COTS modules on DIN rail, with no custom PCB**, so a pilot robot can be wired by one technician in about two days and every part has a datasheet and a replacement on the shelf. A custom power/IO board is a v2 cost-down item once the design is frozen.

## Power (E-01)

- **Pack:** 16S2P LFP (EVE LF105), 51.2 V nominal, 58.4 V full, 40 V cut-off. Orion BMS 2 controls the main contactor K0, the precharge (R1 50 Ω through K0p) and the charge contactor K3. A 500 A shunt measures current, and an insulation monitor checks chassis isolation.
- **Protection chain:** F1 200 A Class T → S1 lockout disconnect (LOTO point) → K0 → 48 V busbar (2 AWG).
- **Branches:** drive 4 × 40 A through safety contactor K1, with VESC battery current limited to 35 A each. Arms 2 × 30 A through safety contactor K2 to the FR5 DC controllers (30-60 V input). 24 V 20 A and 12 V 20 A DIN DC-DCs. BMS/Teensy always-on at 2 A.
- **Peak bus current:** 4 × 35 + 2 × 15 + 25 ≈ 195 A, inside F1 (Class T is time-delayed; motor peaks last seconds).
- **Charging:** dock contacts J1 → F21 125 A → K3, CC/CV at 3.5 kW from a CAN-controlled LFP charger (Delta-Q IC3500 class). The BMS authorises charge only with the pilot pin made and cell temperature 0-45 °C.

## Safety chain (E-02)

- **Dual-channel series loop** through 4 corner E-stops (IP69K), the wireless E-stop receiver relay, the front/rear pressure-sensitive bumper edges, both FR5 safety outputs and the BMS fault relay → Pilz PNOZ s4.
- **Outputs:** K1 (drive bus) drops immediately (stop category 0). K2 (arm bus) drops after 1.0 s so the arms can do a controlled stop first (category 1). Contactor auxiliary contacts feed back to the relay (EDM). Reset is a manual, monitored blue button.
- **Target:** Cat 3 / PL d per ISO 13849-1 for the E-stop function.
- **Normal pauses** use the non-safety path (Jetson → Teensy → VESC zero current, FR5 stop over Ethernet), so the safety contactors are not cycled in normal work.

## Data (E-03)

GbE backbone (Moxa switch) for the lidar, both arm controllers, the 5G router and the Teensy. GMSL2 for the two ZED X through a ZED Link Duo card. USB3 for the two D405s, GNSS and IMU. Three CAN-FD buses on the Teensy: VESCs; BMS + charger; pumps, valves and the EE servo bridge. PTP time sync. **Offline-first:** each robot runs its day plan from a local Field Ledger cache and buffers telemetry when 5G drops.

## Standards and compliance path

| Topic | Standard / rule | v1 approach | Gap to close before selling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Autonomous ag machine safety | ISO 18497 (ANSI/ASABE AD18497) | E-stops, wireless E-stop, bumpers, speed ≤ 1.5 m/s, geofence, beacon; operation in fenced fields with trained staff only | People detection as a **safety function**: add SICK outdoorScan3 safety laser scanners (outdoor-rated, PL d) or a certified equivalent |
| Control-system safety | ISO 13849-1/-2 | PNOZ s4 dual channel, EDM | PL calculation (SISTEMA) with real component B10d values |
| Robot arms | ISO 10218-1/-2, ISO/TS 15066 | FR5 is a collaborative arm; force-limited EE (3.6 N grip) | Risk assessment for arm motion near the aisle; nobody in the aisle while picking |
| Laser weeder | IEC 60825-1, 21 CFR 1040.10 (FDA/CDRH) | Light-tight hood, interlocked skirt, Class 4 inside / Class 1 outside | Product report to CDRH before sale; laser safety officer on staff |
| Electrical | NFPA 79 / UL 3100 (mobile robots) as guidance; UL 1973 for the pack | Fused branches, IP66 enclosure, LOTO disconnect | Pack testing (UN38.3 for shipping, UL 1973 if sold) |
| Food safety | FSMA Produce Safety Rule | Food-contact TPU fingers + silicone cups, washable totes, cleaning SOP | Written sanitation SOP and audit (PrimusGFS / GAP) |
| UAV | FAA Part 107 (+ BVLOS waiver) | Remote pilot certificate, visual observer | BVLOS waiver or the FAA BVLOS rule |
| Radio | FCC Part 15 / carrier certification | Certified modules only (RUTX50, Wi-Fi) | none |
| Pesticide use (SP-1) | FIFRA label, Florida FDACS applicator licence | Licensed applicator supervises | none |
